      How to use this dashboard
      

        

          OSU prohibits P-card purchases of alcohol, cash and ATM transactions, decorations,
          donations and sponsorships, gasoline, gifts and gift cards, insurance, late fees,
          mail and postage, moving expenses, personal purchases, personal memberships,
          salaries and benefits, and service or incentive awards. This dashboard finds
          transactions that may fall into those categories so they can be pulled for testing.
        

        
    
          
  1. Pick the year you are auditing. The audit programme covers 2014; other years are available for comparison.
    2. 
          
  2. Choose a search. The description search reads the merchant's description of what was bought, which is where words like alcohol or gift card show up. The vendor search reads the merchant name, which is how you find purchases from, say, the post office.
    3. 
          
  3. Type a keyword, or click a prohibited category to load keywords that are already known to work for it. Separate several keywords with commas to search for any of them.
    4. 
          
  4. Review the rows, then download the CSV to attach to your working papers.
    5. 
        

        

          A returned row is a lead, not a finding. Descriptions are supplied by the merchant and
          are often generic, and many vendors sell both allowable and prohibited items. Confirm
          each hit against the receipt and the business purpose before concluding anything.
